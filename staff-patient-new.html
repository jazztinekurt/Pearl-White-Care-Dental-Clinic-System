Data privacy consent

We collect the patient's name, birth date, contact details, appointments, treatment records, X-rays and payments, only to treat and bill them. Dentists see clinical records; front desk staff see what they need for booking and billing.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), the patient can ask to view, correct, archive or delete their data.

Ask them. Without one, their records are still kept, and they can create an account later here or on the website.

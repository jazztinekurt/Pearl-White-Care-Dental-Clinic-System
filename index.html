About us

A family clinic that grew up with its patients

Pearl White started in 1999 as the Jena Tan-Booth Dental Clinic, founded by Dr. Jena. For more than 25 years, we have helped families in Caloocan and Quezon City keep healthy teeth and smile with confidence.

As the practice grew and added services, we took the name Pearl White to match a more modern clinic, while keeping the values we started with. Today we run three branches, and online booking lets you see open times without waiting for a reply.

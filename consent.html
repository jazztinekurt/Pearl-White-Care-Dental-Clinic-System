Please read how we handle your information. Open each section to learn more.

What we collect

Your name, date of birth, mobile number, email, the ID or birth certificate you upload, appointments, treatment records, X-rays and payment details. We ask only for what we need to treat and bill you.

Why we use it

To book and manage your visits, keep your dental history across all three branches, send reminders, and record payments and balances.

Who can see it

Only authorized clinic staff, based on their role. Your dentists see your clinical records. Receptionists see what they need for booking and billing. The clinic owner manages accounts and reports and does not see clinical records.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed for your treatment and for legal and billing requirements. Inactive records are archived, not left open.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you can ask to view, correct, archive or delete your data. You can manage these choices later under Privacy settings in your profile.
